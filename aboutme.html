Whatcha Know About Me

I’m a creative nerd at heart—equal parts imagination, curiosity, and joyful chaos. Creativity is the lens through which I experience the world, and curiosity is what keeps me moving forward. When something catches my interest, I tend to fall into it completely, following threads of inspiration wherever they lead. I love asking questions, exploring details, and learning simply for the sake of learning. Those deep dives—whether into ideas, techniques, or concepts—are where I feel most alive and most myself. For me, creativity isn’t a straight line from idea to outcome. It’s messy, exploratory, and full of unexpected turns. I enjoy the process just as much as the result, if not more. Experimentation, trial and error, and playful problem-solving are essential parts of how I create. I’m not afraid to sit with uncertainty, to try something that might not work, or to follow an idea that feels strange or unconventional. Those moments often lead to the most interesting discoveries and the most meaningful growth. I’m also proudly nerdy. I genuinely enjoy learning, researching, and understanding how things work beneath the surface. I find joy in details that others might overlook and satisfaction in refining something until it feels just right. I can get deeply focused on a single concept, obsess over small improvements, and lose track of time while exploring possibilities. That nerdy enthusiasm isn’t something I try to hide—it’s a core part of how I think, create, and connect with the work I do. At the same time, I believe creativity should be fun. I don’t take myself too seriously, and I value humor, playfulness, and a willingness to be silly. Being lighthearted allows me to stay open, flexible, and curious, even when things get challenging. It reminds me that creativity isn’t just about perfection or productivity—it’s about expression, joy, and the freedom to explore without fear. I see silliness as a creative tool, one that keeps ideas fresh and the process enjoyable. I create because it helps me understand the world and my place in it. Making things allows me to process thoughts, emotions, and experiences in ways that words alone sometimes can’t. Each project is a conversation between curiosity and intention, structure and spontaneity. I’m constantly learning—about my craft, about myself, and about how creativity evolves over time. Ultimately, my work is shaped by who I am: curious, imaginative, thoughtful, nerdy, and a little silly. I embrace that combination because it keeps my creativity honest and alive. I’m always growing, always exploring, and always excited to see where the next idea might lead.
